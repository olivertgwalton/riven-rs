# musicbrainz

Music metadata from [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org), with album covers from the
[Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org). It needs no account or key.

An artist is identified by its MusicBrainz artist id, an album by its release group id
and a track by its recording id. A release group can have dozens of releases (the
original CD, reissues, deluxe and regional editions); the album's tracks come from one of
them, the earliest of the releases sharing the commonest tracklist, since that is the
tracklist most releases are named for.

An artist followed in full takes the release group types chosen under *Discography*,
and only the artist's own work: live albums, compilations, remixes, soundtracks and other
secondary types are left out. An album asked for by name is fetched whatever its type.

MusicBrainz serves one request a second to each application, so indexing an artist
takes about a second for each album.
